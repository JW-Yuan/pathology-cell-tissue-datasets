# BACH - ICIAR2018 数据集详情

## 核实后的基本信息

- **名称**：BACH - ICIAR2018
- **年份**：2018
- **器官/组织来源**：Breast
- **染色方式**：H&E
- **任务**：classi、seg
- **数据规模**：400
- **数据内容及标注**：images (4 classes: normal 100, benign: 100, in situ carcinoma: 100, invasive carcinoma: 100) + 20 unlabeled + 10 labeled WSI (10 patients)
- **图像形式**：Patch (classi, 2048x1536) + WSI (semantic seg)

## 数据集简介

ICIAR 2018 BACH: 400 microscopy images for 4-class breast histology classification, plus WSI-level lesion segmentation track.
