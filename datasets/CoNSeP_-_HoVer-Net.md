# CoNSeP - HoVer-Net 数据集详情

## 核实后的基本信息

- **名称**：CoNSeP - HoVer-Net
- **年份**：2019
- **器官/组织来源**：Colorectal adenocarcinoma
- **染色方式**：H&E
- **任务**：seg、classi
- **数据规模**：Train: 27 images, Test: 14 images, 24.319 nuclei
- **数据内容及标注**：images + nuclei (location + class)
- **图像形式**：patch (1000x1000)

## 数据集简介

Colorectal adenocarcinoma histopathology dataset. for seg, classi tasks.
