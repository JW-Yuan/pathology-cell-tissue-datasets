# Gelasca et al. 数据集详情

## 核实后的基本信息

- **名称**：Gelasca et al.
- **年份**：2009
- **器官/组织来源**：Breast
- **染色方式**：H&E
- **任务**：classi、seg
- **数据规模**：58 H&E breast-cancer images on UCSB official distribution page
- **数据内容及标注**：images (malignant/benignant, 1.895 nuclei) + masks
- **图像形式**：Patch (896x768; 768x512)

## 数据集简介

UCSB breast cancer images with benign/malignant labels and segmentation masks; page shows 58 images (50 may refer to a subset).
