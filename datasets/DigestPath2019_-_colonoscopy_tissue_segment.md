# DigestPath2019 - colonoscopy tissue segment 数据集详情

## 核实后的基本信息

- **名称**：DigestPath2019 - colonoscopy tissue segment
- **年份**：2019
- **器官/组织来源**：Colon
- **染色方式**：H&E
- **任务**：seg、classi
- **数据规模**：Train: 660, Test: 212
- **数据内容及标注**：images + lesion annotation
- **图像形式**：patch (avg 5kx5k)

## 数据集简介

DigestPath2019 colonoscopy tissue lesion segmentation and benign/malignant classification subtask; distinct from signet-ring cell detection track.
